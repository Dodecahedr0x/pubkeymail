# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## 1. Introduction

PubKeyMail ("we," "our," or "us") provides a blockchain-based email service that allows users to send and receive emails using their blockchain wallet addresses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Wallet Address Information
- Your blockchain wallet address (public key)
- Blockchain network type (Solana, Ethereum, Polygon)
- Linked wallet addresses (if you connect multiple wallets)

### 2.2 Email Data
- Email content (subject, body text, HTML content)
- Email metadata (sender, recipient, timestamps)
- Attachment metadata (filename, size, type)
- Delivery status and bounce information

### 2.3 Usage Information
- Authentication events (login timestamps, IP addresses)
- API request logs
- Rate limiting counters
- Service usage patterns

### 2.4 Technical Information
- IP address
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Operating system

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

- **Provide Email Services:** Deliver, store, and manage your emails
- **Authentication:** Verify your identity via blockchain signatures
- **Security:** Detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security threats
- **Rate Limiting:** Enforce fair usage limits
- **Compliance:** Meet legal and regulatory obligations
- **Service Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns to improve our service

## 4. Data Retention

### 4.1 Email Retention
- **Free Tier:** Emails are retained for 30 days from receipt
- **Paid Tier:** Emails are retained for 1 year from receipt
- Sent emails are retained indefinitely or until account deletion

### 4.2 Audit Logs
- Security audit logs are retained for 30 days
- May be retained longer for ongoing investigations

### 4.3 Account Data
- Account data is retained while your account is active
- Deleted upon request or account termination

## 5. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

- **With Your Consent:** When you explicitly agree
- **Service Providers:** Third-party services that help us operate (SMTP providers, hosting)
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law, subpoena, or court order
- **Security Purposes:** To investigate fraud or security threats
- **Business Transfers:** In connection with mergers, acquisitions, or asset sales

## 6. Your Rights (GDPR Compliance)

Under GDPR and similar regulations, you have the right to:

### 6.1 Right to Access
Request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

**How to exercise:** Use the Data Export feature at `/api/v1/compliance/export`

### 6.2 Right to Rectification
Request correction of inaccurate personal data.

**How to exercise:** Contact support or update your linked addresses

### 6.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")
Request deletion of your personal data.

**How to exercise:** Use the Account Deletion feature at `/api/v1/compliance/delete`

### 6.4 Right to Data Portability
Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format.

**How to exercise:** Data exports are provided in JSON format

### 6.5 Right to Object
Object to processing of your personal data.

**How to exercise:** Contact us at privacy@pubkeymail.com

### 6.6 Right to Restrict Processing
Request limitation of processing of your personal data.

**How to exercise:** Contact us at privacy@pubkeymail.com

## 7. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures:

- **Encryption:** Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Role-based access to production systems
- **Monitoring:** Security event logging and anomaly detection
- **Brute Force Protection:** Account lockout after failed attempts
- **Rate Limiting:** Protection against abuse

## 8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for international transfers.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

## 10. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information:

- **SMTP Providers:** SendGrid, Postmark, or Mailgun for email delivery
- **Blockchain Networks:** Solana, Ethereum for wallet verification
- **Payment Processors:** Stripe for fiat payments

Each has their own privacy policy governing their use of data.

## 11. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. We do not use tracking cookies for advertising.

## 12. Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information:
- We will notify affected users within 72 hours
- We will notify relevant supervisory authorities as required
- We will provide details about the breach and remediation steps
